# HOD Daily Reports — User Guide

> **For:** All heads of department at Ziwa Rhino And Wildlife Ranch
>
> **Website:** [hoddailyreports.netlify.app](https://hoddailyreports.netlify.app)

---

## Logging in

1. Open the website on your phone or computer
2. Tap your **department** from the grid
3. Tap your **name** (if your department has more than one person, you'll see a list of names)
4. Enter your **password** and tap Log In

Your password is `ziwa2026` (same for everyone).

### If someone else is submitting on your behalf

Every department has a "Someone else" option. The substitute taps "Someone else", types their name, and can go straight to the report — no password needed. Guest access lasts 12 hours.

---

## Submitting a report

After logging in, you land on your **department hub**. This page shows:

- **Date buttons** — the system suggests the right date for you based on the time of day. Before 4 PM, yesterday's report is the main option. After 4 PM, today's is.
- **Recent reports** — any reports you've already submitted, with their status.

To submit:

1. Tap the date button for the report you want to file
2. Fill in every required field (marked with a red asterisk)
3. Tap **Submit Report** at the bottom
4. You'll see a green confirmation when it's done

### Same-day warning

If you tap "Report for today" before 4 PM, the system asks if you meant today or yesterday — this prevents accidental same-day submissions.

---

## Saving a draft

Your form **saves automatically** every 30 seconds as you type. You can also tap **Save Draft** at any time.

If you close the page or lose signal, your work is saved. When you come back and open the same report date, your draft loads automatically.

---

## Editing a submitted report

You can edit a submitted report until **6 PM the day after** the report date. For example, a report for Monday can be edited until 6 PM on Tuesday.

To edit:

1. Go to your department hub
2. Find the report in your recent reports list
3. Tap **Edit** (you'll see a countdown showing how much time is left)
4. Make your changes and tap **Update Report**

After the edit window closes, only management can make changes.

---

## What happens when you're offline

If your phone loses signal while you're working:

- A **red banner** appears at the top: "You're offline. Drafts are saved locally."
- Your draft continues to save on your phone
- If you press Submit while offline, the report is **queued** and will submit automatically when signal returns
- An **amber banner** shows how many reports are waiting to send

You don't need to do anything — just carry on. The system handles it.

---

## Automatic logout

The system logs you out automatically in two situations:

1. **30 minutes of no activity** — if you leave the page idle
2. **6 PM daily** — all sessions end at 6 PM Kampala time

This is to keep the system secure on shared devices. Just log in again when you need to.

---

## Housekeeping — room grid

If you're in Housekeeping, your form shows a grid of all 20 rooms grouped by building. For each room:

- Select **Vacant** or **Occupied**
- If Occupied: choose the room condition (Good / Needs attention / Out of order) and note any damages
- Every room must have a status before you can submit
