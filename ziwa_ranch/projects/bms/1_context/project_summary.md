# BMS — Project Summary

> *Written for people who need to understand and believe in this project, without needing to understand how it works technically. Plain language only.*

---

## What we are building

A management system designed specifically for lodges, hotels, and tourism businesses. One system that handles the daily running of the entire operation — staff reporting, stock and inventory, sales, bookings, communication between teams, and financial tracking — all in one place, all connected, all intelligent.

The system is called BMS: Business Management Software.

---

## The problem it solves

Running a lodge is complex. Information gets lost. Problems are discovered too late. The owner or manager cannot be in every place at once — so things slip through the gaps.

Right now, most lodges in this region use systems like Karibu, which records what happens but does nothing with that information. It is a digital filing cabinet. The manager still has to read every report, chase every department, manually compare numbers, and hope they have spotted the issues before they become expensive problems.

BMS changes this completely. Instead of a system that waits to be asked, BMS actively works for the people running the business. It collects information from every department every day, understands what that information means, and tells management what they need to know — before they have to ask.

---

## How it works

Every department head submits a daily report through the system — what happened, what stock was used, any problems or successes. This takes a few minutes on a phone or computer.

The system takes all of those reports and does the work. It connects the numbers from different departments. It spots patterns. It flags issues. It calculates food costs, tracks stock levels, checks whether reports are consistent. By the time the General Manager opens BMS in the morning, it has already done the analysis and is ready to present what matters.

If the Kitchen is running low on an ingredient, BMS alerts it. If stock is being issued from the store but not showing up in kitchen reports, BMS flags the discrepancy. If a department has been missing its daily reports for three days, BMS makes that visible and uncomfortable.

On the commercial side, BMS handles point of sale for the restaurant and craft shop, tracks all revenue and costs, and builds an accurate picture of the business's financial performance — automatically, from the data the system has already collected.

---

## Who it is built for

BMS is built for **any lodge, hotel, or tourism business** that wants to run their operation properly. It is not built specifically for one business — it is a product that any hospitality business can set up and start using.

**Ziwa Rhino and Wildlife Ranch** is the first business to use it. Ziwa will test and prove every part of the system in real operating conditions before it is offered to others.

---

## What makes it different

Most management systems record information. BMS understands it.

The difference is in what the owner or manager experiences. With a standard system, you log in and see dashboards full of numbers. You still have to do the thinking. With BMS, you log in and the system tells you: here are the three things you need to deal with today, here is why, and here is who is responsible.

That intelligence — built from clean, consistent data collected every single day — is what no other system in this market currently offers.

## Where we are now

BMS is in the planning and design phase. Before a single line of code is written, we are mapping out exactly how the system works, what it needs to do for every type of hospitality business, and what the correct technical foundation looks like to support everything the system will eventually become.

A significant amount of the technology has already been proven through an earlier version of the system — the HOD Daily Reports tool, which has been running live at Ziwa since March 2026 with 16 departments and 30+ staff members using it daily.

BMS takes everything learned from that system and rebuilds it properly — as a product, not just a tool.
